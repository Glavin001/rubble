# Known issues of the generated test vehicles (2026-11-02)

Two vehicles in the acceptance set are generated by Rubble rather than taken from
real content:
- the high-resolution car (`engine_softbody::hires`, "Rubble Saloon HR", exported as
  `vehicles/rubble_hires_jbeam.truck`);
- the procedural sedan (`engine_softbody::procedural`, `vehicles/rubble_sedan_ror.truck`).

Self-made vehicles can carry authoring faults that look like solver faults. The
decision (user, 2026-11-02) is to test on real community vehicles from the Rigs of
Rods repository instead. Both generated vehicles stay in the code, documented here.

## Hi-res car: unstable after a crash, in Rigs of Rods too. Retired from the test set.

Measured 100 km/h into a rigid wall, followed for 30 s:
- **Rubble:** `cargo run --release -p engine-softbody --example explicit_settle -- vehicles/rubble_hires_jbeam.truck scene=wall secs=30`, on branch `feat/explicit-beams-web`.
- **Rigs of Rods:** the unmodified harness, `ror_ref <truck> scale scene=settling/ror-hires/scene.txt frames=N steps=17 roll=1 drag=0`, for N = 300 … 3600.

| | Rubble | Rigs of Rods |
|---|---|---|
| Shape change per 2.5 s after the impact | 45–260 mm RMS, never settling | 89–154 mm RMS until 10 s |
| Broken beams | 329 at 2.5 s, rising to 768 at 30 s | 339 at 2.5 s, 410 at 10 s, then 4,926 of 6,158 at 15 s |
| End | a node 103 m away, moving at 98 m/s, at 30 s | the vehicle blows apart between 10 and 15 s, nodes km away |

Both engines keep pumping energy into the crushed structure. Rigs of Rods diverges
first. So the fault is in the vehicle, not in Rubble's solver.

Likely causes:
- light 2 kg nodes;
- a 6 kN crumple-zone yield threshold;
- capped short-beam stiffness (`stiffness_length` 0.15).

These were tuned to stay stable at rest on a 0.5 ms explicit step. They do not
stay stable once crushed.

**At rest:** the same car breaks 9 and bends 13 beams while just standing. Rigs
of Rods does the same, 3–12 broken.

**Why the acceptance rule missed it:** the rule compares end states 2 s after the
spawn. The hi-res car's accuracy and speed results remain in `accuracy/jbeam_car`
for the record only. The partial speed run is in `inspiration/acceptance-aborted/`,
locally.

Raw results:
- `settling/rubble-hires-idle-wall-drag.txt`: idle and wall, with drag on and off.
- `settling/ror-hires/*.json`: Rigs of Rods node positions and damage at each N.

## Procedural sedan: stable. Heavy breakage. A different variant on the web page.

The same 30 s wall run:

| | Rubble | Rigs of Rods |
|---|---|---|
| Shape change per 2.5 s, 5 s after impact | 4 mm at 5 s, then under 1.3 mm RMS, 12 mm max | 48 mm at 5 s, then 1.3–2.8 mm RMS |
| Broken / bent beams | 354 / 618, constant from 2.5 s | 344 / 540, constant from 2.5 s |

Raw results: `settling/rubble-sedan-wall.txt` and `settling/ror-sedan/*.json`.

Known issues:
- **About 16% of its 2,170 beams break** at 100 km/h. That is much more than a
  real car would shed; the threshold scale was set for a 40 km/h demo. Both
  engines agree, so it is a property of the vehicle, not of the solver.
- **The web page's own soft-solver sedan is a different variant.**
  - It uses the soft solver's tuning, with no 10 kg minimum node mass.
  - On the explicit solver it bends 138 beams at rest and breaks about 57 while
    coasting at 100 km/h.
  - So `softbody.html?solver=explicit` uses the acceptance sedan instead.

## What replaces them

- Real Rigs of Rods repository cars, kept locally under `inspiration/ror-repo/`
  and not committed, because the repository's mods are not GPL unless stated:
  - 1996 Dodge Viper GTS
  - 1988 Audi UR-Quattro
  - Ford Mustang 1979–82
  - BMW E36
- They join Agora S and Agora L.
- Each has to load unmodified in both engines and settle after the wall crash in
  Rigs of Rods before it counts.
- The accuracy rule gains a settling check: 30 s after the impact, the shape
  change per 2.5 s must be within the range Rigs of Rods shows, so that a
  long-run instability cannot pass again.
