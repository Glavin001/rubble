rubble

GPU rigid-body physics with destruction. One Slang solver (AVBD) is compiled to WGSL and runs here in your browser on WebGPU; the same source runs natively on Metal, wgpu and CUDA. Boxes, spheres, capsules, convex hulls, D6 joints, vehicles and fracture, with island sleeping.
