Timber Yard

A two-storey Shardwright timber-frame house (studs, joists, rafters and board cladding, every member split along its grain) and two plank walls, for 24-pounder cannonballs. Rubble's world API, threshold fracture with support loss and splintering, stepped in Rust on WebGPU.

Drag to orbit, click to fire a cannonball where you point. In a raze, turn the director off to fire your own.
