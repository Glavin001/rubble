Does allowing delayed breaks change the conclusion?

Timing agreement counts the same bond breaking within the stated tolerance. “Any frame” only compares whether that bond broke by the end of this run. Neither score alone proves realistic destruction: compare fragment sizes, motion and energy too.

Energy and integration limits

This is an explicit experiment, not a validated production replacement. The model predicts frame endpoints; hidden deformation increments are interpolated across the engine’s contact substeps, and all predicted breaks commit at the frame boundary. It does not yet predict all damping work, support reactions or within-frame fracture. Integration and model errors are therefore mixed. Zero analytic bond evaluations confirms there is no reference stress fallback.
