# Mid-size four-door sedan (the art spec's car)

![front three-quarter](renders/sedan_front34.jpg)

Our own design to the spec's dimensions: framed doors with a hidden B-pillar, rear doors cut round the rear wheel opening, a quarter window in the C-pillar, wrap-round bumper covers, slim headlamps joined by an upper grille, a lower intake, wrap-round tail lamps, 18 in split five-spoke alloys on 235/45 R18.
